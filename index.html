    About Us

    World Tele Communication (WTC) was established on 29 December 2004.
    With over 20 years of industry experience, we have built a strong reputation in mobile phone repair, mobile device sales, and professional POS solutions.

    We specialise in providing complete POS Solutions System, including POS devices, POS software, and business operation solutions tailored for modern businesses.

    Since entering the POS industry in May 2021, we have successfully helped many businesses set up and optimise their POS systems for better efficiency and growth.

    Today, we are proud to be:
    • SalesPlay POS System Distributor
    • Sunmi Authorized Dealer
    • POSPay Authorized Dealer

    Trusted by local businesses across Malaysia, especially in Melaka, we are committed to delivering reliable service, professional support, and long-term business solutions.
