Sonic POS

A practical point-of-sale and business management solution designed for retail businesses that want sales, stock, staff operations and reporting brought together in one system.

Retail POSInventoryFinance ReportsBusiness Management24/7 Online Service

One POS System, More Control

Sonic POS is built to help businesses handle everyday selling and back-office work with clearer records and practical management tools.

Fast Sales & Checkout

Manage products, quantities, open items and checkout from a clear sales screen designed for day-to-day retail operations.

Inventory & Stock Management

Track stock in, stock out, adjustments, suppliers, low-stock situations and movement history so your team can keep a closer eye on inventory.

Sales & Analytics

Review sales performance, orders, items sold, revenue, average sales and top-selling items through structured dashboards and reports.

Finance & Business Reports

Generate detailed sales, product, tax, service-charge and transaction reports, with export options such as PDF and Excel where available in the system.

Staff & Access Management

Support day-to-day operations with staff, membership, access and system-management functions from the POS environment.

Cash Drawer & Shift Records

Keep cash movement and drawer history organised with staff, POS, shift and action records for easier operational checking.
