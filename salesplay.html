About SalesPlay

SalesPlay is a modern cloud-based POS system designed for retail shops, restaurants, cafes and businesses.

It offers a FREE POS application for basic usage, allowing businesses to start operating easily.

Additional advanced features such as inventory management, multi-outlet system, staff control, reports and premium business functions require subscription plans.
