About SalesPlay

SalesPlay is a smart and modern POS system designed for restaurants, cafes, retail shops and many other businesses.

The basic SalesPlay app is free to use, making it easy for small businesses to start digitalising their operations.

Additional advanced features such as inventory management, multi-branch systems, staff control, cloud backup, and premium business tools require subscription plans.

SalesPlay helps businesses improve efficiency, manage sales easier, and support modern cashless payment systems.
